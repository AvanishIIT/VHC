What is Medical Coding?

Medical Coding is the process of accurately documenting and coding patient conditions to reflect their health status and risk profile. It helps healthcare organizations improve risk adjustment accuracy, ensure compliance, and maximize reimbursement.

What is HEDIS Abstraction?

HEDIS Chart Review & Data Abstraction is the process of reviewing medical records and accurately extracting clinical data to support HEDIS quality measures. Our experienced healthcare professionals and certified coders help health plans identify gaps in care, ensure NCQA compliance, improve data accuracy, and maximize quality performance scores.
