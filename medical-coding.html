What is Medical Coding?

Medical Coding is the process of accurately documenting and coding patient conditions to reflect their health status and risk profile. It helps healthcare organizations improve risk adjustment accuracy, ensure compliance, and maximize reimbursement.

Our Capabilities

What We Do

01

Comprehensive HCC Coding & Chart Auditing

Our expert coders meticulously analyze complex clinical charts to capture acute and chronic conditions with absolute precision. We translate raw provider documentation into compliant risk codes while maintaining peak quality control.

  • Hierarchical Condition Category (HCC) Mapping: Precise translation of complex clinical data into accurate risk adjustment codes.
  • MEAT Criteria Validation: Ensuring every logged code strictly meets Medicare guidelines for Monitoring, Evaluating, Assessing, and Treating.
  • Dual-Pass Quality Audits: Implementing rigorous secondary reviews to eliminate coding drops and maximize accuracy.
02

Longitudinal Chart Analysis (Retrospective Reviews)

We perform deep-dive look-back audits on historical medical records to uncover and correct missed diagnostic opportunities. This service bridges the gap between submitted claims and the actual depth of patient care provided.

  • Historical Look-Back Audits: Scanning past records to capture undocumented or dropped chronic conditions.
  • Year-Over-Year Continuity Tracking: Securing annual recaptures of recurring diagnoses across consecutive coverage periods.
  • Discrepancy Remediation: Identifying and resolving gaps between old claims and historical clinical charts.
03

Point-of-Care & Pre-Visit Analytics (Prospective Reviews)

We empower healthcare providers with proactive data before the patient even walks through the clinic door. By analyzing historical insights early, we help clinicians address gaps in care at the exact moment of treatment.

  • Proactive Gaps-in-Care Identification: Alerting providers to potential undocumented conditions prior to the patient encounter.
  • Pre-Visit Chart Preparation: Equipping clinical teams with actionable diagnostic summaries for upcoming appointments.
  • Real-Time Documentation Insights: Supporting point-of-care accuracy to minimize backlogs and late queries.
04

Audit Defense & Compliance Safeguards (RADV Support)

Protect your organization from regulatory risks with our specialized CMS audit readiness services. We validate your data integrity, test your compliance levels, and build rock-solid evidence files to defend against federal reviews.

  • CMS RADV Mock Audits: Simulating federal compliance audits to identify financial risks and coding vulnerabilities.
  • Regulatory Sufficiency Reviews: Confirming that signatures, credentials, and note formats meet strict CMS standards.
  • Appeals & Dispute Documentation: Assembling definitive clinical evidence packages to support and defend contested codes.
